          Questions

          Straight answers.

          Anything else, or call 1-800-481-8638.

          What is a credit, and what is a clip?

          Credits pay for render time. A clip is one 8-second AI video shot. Every render shows its exact credit price before you confirm, and if the video engine hits a technical error and makes no video, the credits come back automatically. Takes you simply don't like are not refunded, which is why every shot can start with a cheap low-res preview.

          What happens after the 12 months?

          From month 13 your plan renews at the regular price, today $299 a month, with the regular 2,880-credit allowance. We email you 30 days before that change, and you can cancel anytime in one click before or after it.

          How do I cancel?

          In one click in your account, anytime. No call, no form. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for, and the first payment has a 14-day money-back guarantee.

          What does the human producer actually do?

          A real producer opens your dashboard within one business day and checks the brand kit, the tone, the links and the plan Genie drafted, then approves it or fixes it. The producer also drafts your press release from your answers, and nothing is published until you approve the text.

          Is "live in minutes" real?

          Yes for the dashboard: your answers become a dashboard built around you right away. The producer pass that polishes it takes up to one business day, which is why we say both every time.

          Who owns what I make? Are AI clips labeled?

          You own your projects, and every project comes with a rights pack. AI-generated clips carry an AI label. Our AI disclosure and terms have the details.

          Does it post to my socials for me?

          Not yet, and we won't pretend otherwise. Today you cut, caption and export for every platform from one panel and share with one tap from your phone. Direct posting rolls out platform by platform as each approves our app.

          What is the free press release?

          A real, published release on our distribution network, drafted from your intake and approved by you. Copies republished on our news site are labeled as a release. It is not an editorial article, and we never promise press coverage.
