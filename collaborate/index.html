Collaborate

Looking for collaborators.

Hollywood to Main Street. Big tech to your living room. If you have a story, let's make it.

Pitch us your idea
Short version: we want to make video with people who have something to say. Hollywood studios, big tech, small businesses, creators, influencers and everyday people with a song or a story. Tell us what you have in mind and a real person writes back within one business day.

Who are we looking for?

  • Studios and filmmakers. Pitch trailers, concept shots, teasers and spots for a release. We're filmmakers too: Madwackd Productions is making its first feature, EARTH.
  • Tech companies. Launch videos, product explainers and the kind of story that makes a feature make sense in thirty seconds.
  • Small businesses. Ads that bring in customers, made at a price that fits a small budget. Start with our 7-step guide to video ads.
  • Creators and influencers. Music videos, branded series, intros and sponsor spots that look like you, not like a template.
  • Everyday people. A music video for your song, a story for your family, a video for a cause you care about.
  • People building a better planet. Environmental and community groups, growers, builders and engineers. That's the founder's vision, and we'd love to help tell those stories.

A note to MrBeast

If you're MrBeast, let's make Zach's decade. Reach out. Our founder, Zach Wennstedt, has one dream collaboration, and it's you. Big ideas, big heart, giving it away: that's the kind of video we want to make. The form below goes straight to us.

How does a collaboration work?

  1. Tell us the idea. A few sentences is plenty: who you are, what you want to make, and when.
  2. A real person replies. Within one business day (Mountain time), from our team, not a bot.
  3. We shape it together. A quick call, then a written plan. If there's a cost, you see the price before any work starts.
  4. We make it. With a producer on it, notes you can give like you would to a crew, and a rights pack at the end.

What we won't do

  • Use a real person's face or voice without their written consent. That includes famous people.
  • Make anything that looks like an endorsement nobody gave.
  • Charge you to "apply" or to pitch us. Sending an idea is free.
