How it works

From idea to finished video.

Eight steps. You approve the price at every one that costs money.

Get early accessCall 1-800-481-8638
1

Pitch the idea

Type it the way you'd say it: “30-second ad for a Denver roofer, upbeat, ends on the phone number.” The AI Producer drafts a shot list with a budget. Nothing is spent yet.

2

Lock your cast

Add each character. Approve one still from the cast sheet and it's locked for every shot.

3

Shape each shot

Edit the shot or leave a director's note. You'll see a clean v2 prompt and a diff, and you approve it.

4

Preview first

A 4-second, low-res take for a few credits shows whether the direction is right before you pay for the full render.

5

Quote, confirm, render

The quote shows seconds × model × takes. Confirm it and the full shots render, inside your project budget.

6

Voice it

Choose a stock voice, upload one, or record yourself in the browser. Your script becomes the voiceover.

7

Export and post

Shots, voice, music, captions and watermark are stitched into one 9:16 or 16:9 file, with AI-content labels in the file data.

8

Keep the receipts

Download the rights pack: prompts, versions, model IDs, dates, consents and charges for the whole project.
