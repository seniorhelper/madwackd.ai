Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026. Effective: November 7, 2026.

This policy explains what personal information Madwackd Productions LLC ("Madwackd," "we," "us") collects through Madwackd Studio™ and www.madwackd.ai (the "Service"), how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored in hashed form) and business name if you add one.
  • Inputs: prompts, scripts, images, video, audio and voice samples you upload, and any consent records you provide for people shown or heard in them.
  • Messages: what you send through our contact form, support chat or producer services.
  • Purchase details: credit packs bought, amounts, dates and billing address. Card numbers are entered directly with Stripe, our payment processor. We do not see or store your full card number.

Information created when you use the Service

  • Outputs (videos and audio generated for you), quotes, render history, credit balance and safety-filter results.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, times and dates, and error logs.
  • Cookies and similar tools that keep you signed in, remember settings, prevent fraud and measure site use. We use Google Analytics to see which pages people visit, roughly where they are (city or country) and what device they use, so we can improve the site. It does not get your name, payment details or the videos you make. To opt out, use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on or block cookies in your browser. We don't use advertising pixels.

Information about other people

If your Inputs include another person's face, voice or name, you must have their consent before you upload them. We use that material only to provide the Service to you.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: quoting, rendering video and voice, delivering Outputs and keeping your credit balance.
  • To process payments, refunds and credit returns, and to prevent fraud and chargeback abuse.
  • To keep the Service safe: running safety filters, enforcing our Acceptable Use Policy, and responding to removal and legal requests.
  • To provide support and producer services, and to send account and service notices.
  • To send marketing emails if you opt in. You can unsubscribe any time.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not use your Inputs or Outputs to train our own AI models, and we do not sell your personal information. We do not use voice samples to identify you; we use them only to create the voice you asked for.

3. How we share information

We share personal information only as needed to run the Service, with:

  • AI model providers, including cloud AI providers for video generation and third-party text-to-speech voice providers, who process prompts and media to create your Outputs under their own terms. We describe these providers by category; you can ask us for the current list.
  • Stripe, for payments and fraud prevention.
  • Hosting, storage, email and support vendors that work on our behalf under confidentiality duties.
  • Producers or contractors you choose to work with through our producer services.
  • Authorities or others when the law requires it, to answer valid legal process, to protect people from harm, or to report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as required by law.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold, merged or reorganized, subject to this policy.

4. How long we keep information

  • Account and purchase records: while your account is open, then as long as needed for tax, accounting and legal purposes (generally up to 7 years for payment records).
  • Inputs and Outputs: until you delete them or close your account, plus up to 30 days in backups.
  • Safety and abuse records, including blocked prompts and removal requests: as long as needed to enforce our policies and meet legal duties.

Closing and deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself: sign in to the studio, open "Account and billing," and use "Delete my account." You will be asked to type a confirmation phrase and to have signed in within the last 10 minutes. When you confirm:

  • Your plan is canceled immediately. The current month is not refunded (see Credits and Refund Policy). Unused credits are lost.
  • Deleted: your profile and sign-in record, your projects, quotes, renders and video files, voiceovers, uploaded photos and voice samples, Madi conversations and plans, Studio boards, posts, feeds, hubs, API keys and webhooks, and any sales inquiries you sent while signed in or with the same email.
  • Kept, without your name, email, IP address or prompt text: payment and credit ledger entries, orders and payment-event records (for tax, accounting and dispute purposes, generally up to 7 years); consent records, kept as a version number and fingerprint only; safety and takedown records (category and date). These keep a random account ID that no longer points to a profile.
  • Not covered by this button: Stripe keeps its own payment records under its own policy, and copies in backups can remain for up to 30 days. Security logs are kept as described in item 3 above.
  • Closing is permanent: the same account can't be reopened. If anything is on hold for a safety or legal review, we can't delete it yet and will tell you so.

Can't sign in? Use the support page and choose "Privacy request."

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal information and get access to it.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your information (you can do this yourself in the studio, see section 4).
  • Get a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not sell personal information or use it for those kinds of profiling.
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights.

How to make a request: use the contact form on our website and choose "Privacy request." We will verify your identity before acting. You can use an authorized agent where the law allows. We respond within 45 days, and may extend once by 45 more days when reasonably necessary, with notice.

Colorado residents: If we deny your request, you may appeal by using the contact form and choosing "Privacy appeal." We will respond to appeals within 45 days. If you disagree with our answer, you may contact the Colorado Attorney General.

California residents: You have the rights described above under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended. In the last 12 months we collected the categories described in Section 1 (identifiers, commercial information, internet activity, audio and visual information, and inferences only as needed for safety) for the purposes in Section 2, and shared them only with the service providers described in Section 3. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We honor these rights for every user, whether or not a given law applies to a business our size.

Global Privacy Control: We honor browser opt-out signals such as Global Privacy Control as a request to opt out of any sale or targeted advertising.

6. Age limit

The Service is only for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us through the contact form.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and payment handling by Stripe, which is certified as a PCI Level 1 service provider. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Where information is processed

We are based in the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. By using the Service you understand your information may be processed outside your home country.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make a material change, we will post it here and notify you by email or on the website before it takes effect.

10. Contact

Madwackd Productions LLC, 1001 Bannock St, Suite 660, Denver, CO 80204. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form on our website.
