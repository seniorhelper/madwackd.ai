## 1. The honest picture

ElevenLabs is a voice-AI platform with (per its own site today) text to speech, voice cloning and design, music, sound effects, dubbing, speech to text, voice agents, an API and an "Image & Video" product that runs on third-party video models. It claims 17,500+ voices and 90+ languages. Plans checked today: Free $0 (10k credits, about 10 minutes of audio), Starter $6, Creator $22, Pro $99, Scale $299, Business $990 a month; commercial license from Starter up.

We will not outrank ElevenLabs for "text to speech", "AI voice generator" or "voice cloning". Those SERPs are theirs plus Canva, Clipchamp, VEED, Speechify and Adobe. Fighting there is wasted money.

Where we can win is the **intersection** nobody owns: a person who needs a finished video with a voice, a price they can see before anything renders, and a human to call. ElevenLabs sells minutes of audio. Madwackd sells finished video with the voice built in. Every page in this plan is built on that difference, stated as fact, never as "better".

### ElevenLabs gaps relative to Madwackd (verifiable today)

### Where ElevenLabs is simply bigger (say so on the page)

Voice count, languages, API, dubbing, voice agents, voice cloning live today. Madwackd has a small curated set of voice styles (narrator, trailer, upbeat promo, friendly neighbor, documentary), custom voices are the next voice feature, and the studio is in early access. Saying this builds trust and keeps the comparison page out of the "biased vendor listicle" bucket the SERP is full of.
