    FAQ

    Questions, answered straight.

    What is Madwackd Studio?

    An AI video studio that turns an idea into a finished video with voice: ads, trailers, music videos, explainers and social clips. You see the price before every render, keep characters consistent from shot to shot, and download a rights pack for every project. It's in early access.

    Is it only for ads?

    No. Ads are a big part of it, but the same workflow makes movie trailers, music videos, explainers, product demos and short social clips in 9:16 and 16:9.

    How do I know what a render will cost?

    Every render starts with a quote: seconds times model times takes, in credits. You confirm it before anything is spent, each project has a budget cap. Prompts our safety check blocks cost nothing, and if the video engine hits a technical error and makes no video, the credits come back automatically.

    What if I don't like a shot?

    Takes you don't like aren't refunded, so start with a low-cost, low-res 4-second preview before the full render. Fix the direction with director's notes and another cheap preview, then render the full shot.

    Can the video have a voice?

    Yes, from day one. Pick a stock voice, upload a voice, or record your own in the browser. Custom voices need a consent check: your own voice, or written permission from the speaker.

    Which AI makes the video?

    Third-party, generally available AI video models from a major cloud provider, not preview versions. Every clip carries an invisible provenance mark that identifies it as AI-generated.

    Can I put a real celebrity in my video?

    No. Requests for a real person's name or likeness are blocked. Use original characters, your own product, or people who've signed a release.

    Can a person produce it for me?

    Yes. Hire a Madwackd producer: Junior, Senior or Executive packages, from a single social ad to a full trailer. See human producers.
