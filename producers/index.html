Producer questions

How much does a human producer cost?

Done-for-you commercial spots start at $2,500, music videos at $5,000 and high-end commercials at $20,000. The price depends on length, shots and revisions, and you get a quote before work starts.

Can I talk to a producer first?

Yes. Request a free 30-minute call. It is a request, not a confirmed booking: a person reviews every request and confirms within one business day, and calls go to projects that are a fit.
