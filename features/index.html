Director's notes

Your note changes the shot, not the sentence.

Most tools glue your feedback onto the end of the prompt until it's a mess. We keep each shot as clean fields (subject, expression, camera, light, pacing, audio) and rewrite only what your note touches.

You see v1 and v2 side by side, approve it, and only then spend a credit. Don't like v3? Revert to v2 in one click.

v1 A friendly plumber holds a wrench in a bright kitchen.

Note: “make him smile more, and get closer”

v2 Slow push-in to a close-up. A friendly plumber holds a wrench in a bright kitchen. Expression: a broad, warm, natural smile.

Character Lock

Same face in shot one and shot twenty.

Describe a character or upload a reference, then approve one still from its cast sheet. Each shot's opening frame is built from that locked still, and the video starts from it. That's how the face stays the same.

Original characters only. We don't recreate real people.
