ElevenLabs vs Madwackd Studio: voice platform or video studio?

By Zach Wennstedt, founder of Madwackd Studio · Facts checked on November 8, 2026

Short answer: ElevenLabs is a voice platform. You get audio: text to speech, cloned voices, dubbing, music, sound effects and voice agents, and an image and video product that runs on other companies' video models. Madwackd Studio is a video studio with the voiceover built in: you get a finished clip, you see the credit price before every render, and a human producer is one phone call away. If you need audio files, pick ElevenLabs. If you need a finished video with a voice and a price you can see first, that is what Madwackd Studio is for.

This page is written by the founder of Madwackd Studio, so read it knowing that. Every ElevenLabs fact below comes from elevenlabs.io as it read on November 8, 2026, with no claims beyond what their own pages say. ElevenLabs is a trademark of its owner; we use the name only to identify their product. Prices change; check both pricing pages before you buy.

What each one actually is

ElevenLabs describes itself around voice: text to speech with 17,500+ voices in 90+ languages (their numbers), voice cloning and voice design, dubbing, speech to text, music, sound effects, a voice-agent platform and an API. Its home page also lists an "Image & Video" product that uses third-party video models. Plans are sold as monthly credits that roughly equal minutes of audio.

Madwackd Studio is an AI video studio from a Denver production company. You describe a shot, the studio shows the credit estimate (seconds x model x takes) before anything renders, you approve it, and the clip comes back with sound. Voiceovers come from a curated set of stock voice styles inside the same project. Real producers run whole projects for people who would rather not do it themselves, and nine free Creator Studio tools run in your browser with no account.

Where ElevenLabs is bigger

If your job is audio, ElevenLabs has more of everything: more voices, more languages, voice cloning you can use today, dubbing, a developer API and a voice-agent product. Podcasters, app builders, audiobook narrators and anyone building a phone agent should start there. Madwackd Studio does not try to be a general text-to-speech service, and we are still in early access.

Where Madwackd Studio is different

  • The voice is attached to a finished video. You never export audio from one tool and line it up in another. Shot, voiceover and export live in one project.
  • You see the price first. Every render shows its credit estimate before you confirm, and each project has a spending cap. Prepaid credits mean the balance never goes below zero.
  • People, not just plans. A junior, senior or executive producer can run the whole thing, quoted up front. Commercial spots start at $2,500, music videos at $5,000.
  • Consent first. No custom voice is made until you confirm it is your own voice or you hold written permission, and that consent is stored with the project.
  • Free tools before any money. The Creator Studio hook writer, script fitter, caption maker and six more run on your device with no sign-up.

Which one should you pick?

  • You need narration for a podcast, audiobook, app or phone agent. ElevenLabs. Madwackd does not sell standalone audio.
  • You need a 15 to 30 second ad or social clip with a voice, and you want to know the cost before you start. Madwackd Studio. Fit your script with the free script fitter, then run a $5 Test Drive.
  • You want someone to make the whole thing. Madwackd producers, quoted up front. ElevenLabs does not list a done-for-you service.
  • You already have a cloned voice you love. Keep using it where its license allows. For Madwackd videos, use the stock styles; custom voices are not offered today.

Questions people ask

Is Madwackd Studio an ElevenLabs alternative?

Only if you need a finished video. ElevenLabs is a voice platform: text to speech, voice cloning, dubbing, music and voice agents, plus an image and video product on third-party models. Madwackd Studio is a video studio with the voiceover built in, a credit quote before every render and human producers you can call. If you need audio files for a podcast, an app or a voice agent, ElevenLabs is the better fit.

Can I use a voiceover I made in ElevenLabs inside Madwackd Studio?

Madwackd Studio's voice layer is built around its own stock voice styles and consent-checked custom voices, so we do not advertise importing third-party voice files. If you have audio you must keep, call a producer and we will tell you honestly whether it fits the project. Any audio you bring is subject to its own license terms.

Does Madwackd Studio clone voices?

Not today. Stock voice styles (narrator, trailer, upbeat promo, friendly neighbor, documentary) are live, and no real person's voice is cloned. Any custom voice, when it is offered, needs a consent check: your own voice, or written permission from the speaker. We never make voices of celebrities or public figures.

What does it cost to try each one?

ElevenLabs lists a Free plan with 10,000 credits a month (about 10 minutes of audio) and a Starter plan at $6 a month, checked on elevenlabs.io/pricing on November 8, 2026. Madwackd Studio has free Creator Studio tools with no card, and a $5 Test Drive with 50 credits, about two 8-second shots at 720p, one per account.

Does ElevenLabs make video?

Its site lists an Image & Video product that runs on third-party video models, alongside its voice products. It does not describe a per-job price quote before a render, human producers or a done-for-you service. Check elevenlabs.io for current features; this page reflects November 8, 2026.
