What each room does

Make it. Pitch it. Post it. Get paid.

Earnings desk

Turn the dial to set a monthly goal. Log every payment by source: tips, brand deals, video sales, client work, platform payouts. The needle and six income meters move as you go, and the pace line shows what a day it takes to hit the goal. Export it all to CSV.

Pitch room

Answer a few questions and get a logline. Add comps, audience and your ask. Rehearse against a 60-second, 3-minute or 10-minute clock with cue cards, then print a one-sheet or copy the pitch.

Publish desk

Switch on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest and Threads. Write one caption and watch each platform's length meter. The kit copies your caption and opens each upload page. Direct posting rolls out platform by platform as each platform approves our app.

Mailing list

Add fans, brands and crew with tags, or import a CSV. Write the email and open it in your own email app with everyone in BCC. Your list never leaves your browser.

Tip jar

Add Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, Patreon or a Stripe payment link. Get tip buttons, link-in-bio text and a one-file tip page you can host anywhere. We take 0%.

Marketing bay and experts

Free press release and bio generators, the nine free Creator Studio tools, and one-click launches to our network's paid services: SEO, conversion fixes, speed-to-lead, press releases and more. Plus producers, masterclass and mentorship.
