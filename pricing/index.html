    Our fair-render policy.

    Preview first

    Start with a 4-second, low-res preview for a few credits. If the direction is wrong, fix it before you pay for the full render.

    Technical failures refunded

    If the video engine hits a technical error and makes no video, the credits come back automatically. Prompts our safety check blocks never cost anything.

    Safety blocks aren't refunded

    If the engine's safety filter blocks a finished video, the credits aren't refunded. Three blocks in 24 hours pauses the account.

    Pricing questions

    Are these the real prices?

    Yes. These are the prices you pay today. The studio still shows the exact credits for every shot before you confirm.

    What's a credit?

    Credits pay for render time. They cost $0.09 to $0.12 each, depending on your plan or pack. Every quote shows the credits before you confirm.

    What's refunded?

    If the video engine hits a technical error and makes no video, your credits come back automatically. Prompts our safety check blocks are stopped before any charge. Videos the engine's safety filter blocks, and takes you don't like, aren't refunded. That's why every shot can start with a cheap 4-second preview.

    Do I need credits first?

    Yes. Every render needs prepaid credits and a confirmed quote. Balances never go below zero.

    Do credits expire?

    We'll publish credit terms before launch.
