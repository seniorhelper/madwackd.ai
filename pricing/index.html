      Pricing questions

      Are these the real prices?

      Yes. These are the prices you pay today. The studio still shows the exact credits for every shot before you confirm.

      What's a credit?

      Credits pay for render time. They cost $0.10 to $0.14 each, depending on your plan or pack. Every quote shows the credits before you confirm.

      What's refunded?

      If the video engine hits a technical error and makes no video, your credits come back automatically. Prompts our safety check blocks are stopped before any charge. Videos the engine's safety filter blocks, and takes you don't like, aren't refunded. That's why every shot can start with a cheap 4-second preview.

      Do I need credits first?

      Yes. Every render needs prepaid credits and a confirmed quote. Balances never go below zero.

      Do credits expire?

      Credits you buy in a pack don't expire while your account is open. Unused monthly plan credits roll over for 1 month. See the credits and refund policy.
