    Pricing questions

    Are these final prices?

    No. They're early-access placeholders to show how pricing will work. Final pricing comes at launch, and early members get founding rates.

    What's a credit?

    A credit is $0.10 of render time. Every quote shows credits and dollars before you confirm.

    What's free?

    Any render that's blocked or fails: you're never charged for failures. Low-res 4-second previews cost a few credits.

    What's the re-roll rule?

    If a full render has a technical fault (warped face or hands, a character that drifts from its locked look, broken audio), flag it and you get one re-roll credit for that shot. Creative changes go through director's notes and a preview.

    Do credits expire?

    We'll publish credit terms before launch.
